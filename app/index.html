⚠ The one trophy that might be missed

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. After the mission Down to Earth in Chapter 1, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning, follow her to the balcony and meditate there before Chapter 1 ends. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam. Sources: PowerPyx via KeenGamer, GTABase, GTA Vibes.

Stranger mission strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) are also reported to have windows tied to story progress — do each strand’s missions as they appear.

Roadmap to the platinum

Four stages, in order. The platinum is Vice Got a Price! and pops when the other 54 are done. All 55 trophies are offline and single-player — there are no online trophies.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1–4, the final mission, and the seven named story missions. Do the Lucia balcony meditation in Chapter 1. Expect roughly 13 story trophies here.

  2. Stage 2 — Side content as it opens

    Stranger strands, partner crime, activities and the small moment trophies. Do stranger missions as they appear, not after the story.

  3. Stage 3 — Post-story cleanup

    Free roam after the credits: stunts, shooting ranges, drone races, races, collectibles, vaults, gangs and money trophies. The 100% checklist is open now (Touch Pad > Progress > Stats > 100% Completion).

  4. Stage 4 — 100% completion, then platinum

    Clear the whole 100% checklist for A Long Road (gold). The platinum pops with it.

About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it on 18 November 2026: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden. The guides are written for this entry in our own words and cite their sources beside each trophy — PowerPyx, Push Square, KeenGamer, EGamers, HowToVideoGame, GTA Vibes, GamesFuze, Insider Gaming and Vice City Hustle. Where no published method exists yet, the guide says so honestly instead of inventing a step. No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used anywhere: the look is our own.
